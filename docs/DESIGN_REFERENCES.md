## LingoLeap

Reference: [TOEFL 2026 Complete the Words](https://app.lingoleap.ai/toefl/reading?task=reading_26_complete_words).

The public catalog showed section/mock navigation, source groupings, task filters, search, question cards, and section entry points. Initially, opening an item required login; at that stage no authenticated interaction was claimed or copied.

The local application adopted a separate task bank with four sections and twelve task filters, plus a compact exam list with direct whole-test and R/L/W/S actions. Strict mocks remain distinct from guided practice so pause, replay, and explanations do not enter strict flow. Third-party layout is not authority for TOEFL timing, counts, or scoring.

## Itongzhuo

Reference: [IELTS listening catalog](https://ielts.itongzhuo.com/business/ielts/student/jumpIeltsQuestionList.do?sSubjects=0&type=1&aiType=0).

The public page showed task/scenario filters, a book/Test/Part matrix, and not-started/in-progress/completed status legends. The local design used clear status and direct section access, without retaining card/matrix toggles. Detailed progress, verification, and source information live outside the minimal home list. IELTS tasks, timing, and marketing claims were not applied to TOEFL.

## Local design principles

- Keep independent application branding and avoid third-party logos, marketing, account, or payment features.
- Compare cloze, fixed sentence slots, navigation, review, and clocks against supplied examination screenshots and verified rules.
- Source learning content from local authorized materials, not downloaded third-party question banks.
- Keep filters, scoring, and review usable offline without cloud AI grading.
- Show the difference between strict and assisted modes before starting.

## Authenticated task-page review
