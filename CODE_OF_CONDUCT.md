# Code of conduct

English | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

We want contributors and learners to participate regardless of background, language, disability, experience, or identity. Treat others with respect and focus disagreement on ideas and evidence.

Expected behavior includes constructive feedback, acknowledging mistakes, respecting privacy and copyright, and making room for people who are learning. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publishing someone's private materials or recordings without permission are not acceptable.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project. If behavior causes harm, stop the interaction and report it privately to a repository maintainer through a verified contact channel. If no private channel is listed, ask for one without publishing the person's private details. Do not retaliate against a report made in good faith.

Maintainers may remove content, ask for a correction, restrict participation, or ban a participant based on severity and repeated behavior. They should consider context, protect reporter privacy as far as practicable, and explain actions to the affected person when appropriate. Participants may request reconsideration through the same private channel. This policy does not establish an emergency response service; contact appropriate local services in an emergency.
